# Kinmap — Genogram Editor

Kinmap is a free tool for building **genograms** — the family-tree-style
diagrams therapists, counselors, and researchers use to map out family
relationships across generations. It runs entirely in your web browser,
with no account, subscription, or internet connection required after
you've downloaded it.

## Overview

A genogram is like a family tree, but richer: alongside births,
marriages, and divorces, it can show emotional relationships between
people — who's close, who's in conflict, who's estranged, and more.
Kinmap gives you a simple visual canvas for building these diagrams by
hand, and automatically arranges everyone into tidy generational rows as
you add people, so you don't have to manually position each box.

Everything you create is saved directly in your browser (nothing is
sent to a server), and you can export your work at any time so you
never lose it.

## Features

- **Add family members** — name, sex, and status (e.g. index patient,
  deceased) for each person on the tree.
- **Automatic layout** — Kinmap arranges everyone into generation rows
  and keeps couples centered over their children automatically, so the
  diagram stays readable as your family tree grows.
- **Partner relationships** — connect two people with a marriage,
  engagement, cohabitation, separation, divorce, widowhood, and more.
- **Emotional ties** — draw connecting lines that show the *quality* of
  a relationship between two people: close, fused, conflict, distant,
  cutoff, abuse, and other standard genogram relationship types.
- **Communities** — group members together (e.g. a household or
  extended family unit) and add notes to a group.
- **Notes** — attach freeform notes to any individual or group.
- **Labels & highlighting** — tag members with custom labels, then
  highlight everyone who shares a label to spot patterns at a glance.
- **Undo / redo** — step backward and forward through your edits.
- **Search** — quickly find a specific family member in a large tree.
- **Zoom & fit-to-screen** — navigate large family trees easily.
- **Save, open, and export** — save your work in the browser, reload it
  later, or export it as a file you can back up or share.

## Install and Use (via ZIP download)

Kinmap doesn't require any installation, coding tools, or technical
setup. You just download it and open it in your browser.

1. **Download the project**
   - On the Kinmap GitHub page, click the green **`<> Code`** button.
   - Choose **Download ZIP** from the dropdown menu.
   - The ZIP file will download to your computer (usually into your
     `Downloads` folder).

2. **Unzip the file**
   - **Mac**: Double-click the downloaded `.zip` file. It will unzip
     into a folder automatically.
   - **Windows**: Right-click the downloaded `.zip` file and choose
     **Extract All…**, then choose a location and click **Extract**.

3. **Open Kinmap**
   - Open the unzipped folder.
   - Find the file named **`index.html`**.
   - Double-click it. It will open automatically in your default web
     browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox all work), and you're
     ready to start building your genogram.

4. **Save your work**
   - Kinmap automatically saves your progress in your browser as you
     work, so you can close the tab and come back later on the same
     computer and browser.
   - For a permanent backup (or to move your work to another computer),
     use the **Export** button in the toolbar to save your genogram as
     a file you can store anywhere, and the **Save**/**Open** buttons to
     manage multiple saved genograms.

> **Tip:** Keep the whole unzipped folder together — `index.html` needs
> the other files in that folder (the styling and program files) to
> work correctly. Don't move `index.html` out on its own.

### Updating to a newer version

If a newer version of Kinmap is released, repeat the steps above to
download and unzip the latest version into a new folder. Use the
**Export** button in your current copy first if you want to carry your
existing genogram over, then **Open** that exported file in the new
version.
